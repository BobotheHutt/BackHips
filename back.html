Lower Back Basics

The lower spine is supported by small bone blocks separated by rubbery, fluid-filled shock absorbers called discs. When these spaces experience heavy, constant pressure from sitting or standing out of alignment, it can squeeze the nearby nerve roots that travel from your spine down into your legs.

🔧 Standard Back Routines

Cat-Cow Mobility Drill
10 Movements

Get on your hands and knees. Slowly let your stomach sink down toward the floor while looking up slightly. Then, gently round your back up toward the ceiling like an arching cat while tucking your chin. Keep it fluid and smooth.

Bird-Dog Core Stability
12 Alternating Reps

On hands and knees, tighten your midsection. Slowly raise and straighten your right arm straight ahead while pushing your left leg straight back behind you. Hold for 2 seconds, drop down, and switch sides to brace the core.
