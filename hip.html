Think of the hip joint like a heavy load-bearing ball-and-socket setup. When the joint gets tight or loses its natural cushioning, the surrounding muscles freeze up like a defensive shield to protect it. These exercises help unlock that tightness.

Glute Bridges
10 Reps / 3 Second Hold

Lie flat on your back with knees bent. Push firmly through your heels to raise your hips until your body forms a straight line from your shoulders to your knees. Squeeze the muscles at the top to build stability around the joint socket.

Seated Chair Stretch
3 Sets / 30 Second Hold

Sit upright in a solid, firm chair. Cross one ankle flat over the opposite knee to make a "figure 4". Keeping your back straight, lean your upper body slightly forward until you feel a comfortable pull in the outer hip muscle.

Massage Gun
Low Speed Setting / 2 Minutes

Float the gun head gently along the thick, fleshy muscle sheets on the outside of your thigh or the upper rear pocket. Do not press hard over any solid bone spots. Let the vibration work to loosen tight muscle shielding.

Foam Roller Column
Slow Passes / 90 Seconds

Rest the side or rear of your hip right onto the roller tube. Use your arms to slowly roll over tight areas. When you hit a tender spot, hold still for a few seconds to let the pressure loosen up the knot.
